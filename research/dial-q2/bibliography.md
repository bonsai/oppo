# Dial Q2 — Books & Research Bibliography

## Core / highest priority

### 1. 富田英典『声のオデッセイ：ダイヤルQ2の世界―電話文化の社会学』
- 恒星社厚生閣, 1994-05
- 159 pages
- ISBN 978-4-7699-0768-8
- Core themes: Dial Q2 system, social problem, telephone communication, youth culture, Q2 communication, commercialization of voice.
- Why important: the central sociological monograph specifically devoted to Dial Q2.
- Source: 版元ドットコム / Tokyo Metropolitan Library catalog.

### 2. 富田英典「若者のパーソナル・コミュニケーションの変容―NTT『ダイヤルQ2』を手がかりに」
- 『神戸山手女子短期大学紀要』35, pp.33-67, 1992-12-20
- Repository: Kansai University Academic Repository
- Open PDF listed by repository.
- Why important: contemporaneous academic study; directly connects Q2 with transformation of young people's personal communication.

### 3. 富田英典『インティメイト・ストレンジャー：「匿名性」と「親密性」をめぐる文化社会学的研究』
- 関西大学出版部, 2009-03
- ISBN 978-4-87354-475-5
- 356 + xi + 25 pages
- Part I includes: transformation of telephone communication, characteristics of communication using Dial Q2, and the concept of the “intimate stranger.”
- Why important: gives a later theoretical framework for interpreting the Q2 experience rather than treating it only as a historical curiosity.

## Telephone / media context

### 4. 吉見俊哉・若林幹夫・水越伸『メディアとしての電話』
- 弘文堂, 1992
- Why important: broader telephone-media history and theory. Contemporary discussion places Dial Q2 and telephone culture within the transformation of the telephone from a functional device into a medium of conversation and self-expression.

### 5. 富田英典・藤本憲一・岡田朋之・松田美佐・高広伯彦『ポケベル・ケータイ主義！』
- ジャストシステム, 1997
- Why important: useful bridge from Q2 / telephone culture to pager and mobile-phone culture.

### 6. 富田英典『みんなぼっちの世界』 (共編著)
- 恒星社厚生閣, 1999
- Why important: useful for the transition from anonymous communication to later mobile / networked forms.

## Legal / consumer-protection research

### 7. 清水巌・円山茂夫「ダイヤルQ2取引の実態と消費者保護―電話による情報提供契約論」
- 『法律時報』64巻, 1992
- Serialized across 1992 issues; NDL index identifies parts 1–4.
- Part 1: 1992-04
- Part 2: 1992-05
- Part 3: 1992-06
- Part 4 (final): 1992-08
- Why important: contemporaneous legal analysis of Q2 transactions and consumer protection.

### 8. 本田守弘「ダイヤルQ2を利用したわいせつ音声情報の提供とわいせつ物公然陳列罪の成否―大阪地裁平成3年12月2日判決」
- 『法律のひろば』45(3), pp.47-51, 1992-03
- Why important: shows how Q2 became a legal problem involving paid voice information and public-obscenity law.

### 9. 紀藤正樹「ダイヤルQ2がなぜ被害を生み続けるのか―NTTの悪徳商法」
- 『消費者法ニュース』48, pp.101-104, 2001-07
- Why important: consumer-protection perspective following the Supreme Court Dial Q2 ruling.

### 10. 渡辺達徳「判例紹介 消費者契約と信義則に関する3つの最高裁判決」
- 『クレジット研究』38, pp.213-225, 2007-03
- Includes discussion of the Dial Q2 call-charge case (Supreme Court, 2001-03-27).

## Commercial / contemporary guide books — useful as primary cultural artifacts

### 11. 小林活夫・SADJA『ダイアルQ2徹底ガイド―利用法から儲け方まで360゜活用マニュアル』
- 大陸書房, 1991-04
- 213 pages
- ISBN 978-4-8033-3308-4
- Why important: not academic, but valuable evidence for what Q2 looked like as a contemporary commercial medium and how users/providers were instructed to think about it.

## Research directions

1. **Communication sociology** — anonymity, intimacy, youth culture, personal communication.
2. **Media history** — household telephone → Q2 → pager → mobile → Internet.
3. **Consumer/legal history** — billing, consent, minors, information contracts.
4. **Sexuality / urban culture** — voice, anonymity, telephone clubs, commercialization.
5. **Memory studies** — compare contemporaneous documents with later first-person recollections.
6. **Business history** — paid information, content providers, NTT's information-service strategy.
7. **Material culture** — household telephone, public telephone, magazines, advertisements, dialing practices.

## Priority reading order for oppo / Dial P2

`1 富田1994 → 2 富田1992 → 3 富田2009 → 4 電話メディア → 6/5 mobile transition → 7–10 legal → 11 contemporary guide`

The key methodological point: **do not use the later theoretical interpretation as evidence for what users actually remembered.** Keep contemporary evidence, later scholarship, and retrospective memory in separate RAG layers.
