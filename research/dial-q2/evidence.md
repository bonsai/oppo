# Dial Q2 — Evidence Seed

> Research corpus for oppo / Dial P2. This file prioritizes public primary or institutional sources. It is a seed corpus, not a complete history.

## 1. Service definition and period

NTT East/West state that Dial Q2 was provided from **July 1989 (Heisei 1)** as an **information-fee collection agency service**. Providers supplied paid programs through numbers beginning with **0990**, and NTT collected the information fee from users on behalf of the provider.

Source: NTT East/West, 2011-11-15, service termination announcement.
https://www.ntt-east.co.jp/release/detail/20111115_01.html

## 2. End of service

NTT East/West announced that new applications would end on 2011-12-15 and the Dial Q2 service would end on **2014-02-28**, citing the diversification of information-delivery methods such as the Internet and declining use.

Source: NTT East/West, 2011-11-15.
https://www.ntt-east.co.jp/release/detail/20111115_01.html

NTT West repeated the final termination notice in February 2014. Disaster-donation programs using the mechanism were replaced by a separate disaster-donation service from 2014-03-01.

Source: NTT West, 2014-02-01.
https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/info/support/dialq2.html

## 3. High-bill / family-use problem

A May 12, 1993 House of Representatives Telecommunications Committee record discusses a case in which a person received a **2.4 million yen** high charge through Dial Q2. The same record discusses litigation concerning a child using Dial Q2 without the parent's knowledge and disputes over information and call charges.

Source: National Diet Library, Diet Proceedings Search System, House of Representatives Telecommunications Committee, 126th Diet, 7th session, 1993-05-12.
https://kokkai.ndl.go.jp/simple/detail?current=-1&minId=112604816X00719930512

## 4. Internet connection / dialer problem

NTT documented a later class of incidents in which users downloaded software while using the Internet and were subsequently connected to Dial Q2 numbers, producing high information charges. NTT introduced a connection-check program and later moved many Internet-related programs into a password-protected category.

Sources:
- NTT West, 2001-01-30.
https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/0101/010130.html
- NTT West, 2001-04-24 summary.
https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/0107/010726b_1.html

## 5. Service design and categories

NTT's 2001 material describes Dial Q2 as something valued because it was easy and convenient to use. The service had open and password-protected categories; the 2001/2002 revisions included age restrictions for the password category and changes to fee limits.

Source: NTT West, 2001-07-26.
https://www.ntt-west.co.jp/news/0107/010726b.html

## Research interpretation — keep separate from evidence

These sources support studying Dial Q2 not only as a billing controversy but as an early Japanese paid-information medium attached to the household telephone. That interpretation is a research hypothesis and should not be presented as a direct statement by NTT or the Diet.
