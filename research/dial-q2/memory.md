# Dial Q2 Memory RAG — collection memo

## Purpose

Collect **how people remember Dial Q2**, without treating memories as automatically factual. The research object is the memory itself: what was used, how it was discovered, what emotions surrounded it, and what happened afterward.

## Evidence levels

- A — primary/institutional contemporaneous evidence: NTT documents, Diet proceedings, court decisions, newspapers/magazines from the period
- B — contemporaneous secondary sources: books, magazines, advertisements, interviews published at the time
- C — first-person retrospective: blogs, note, SNS, interviews, oral histories
- D — hearsay / second-hand recollection

## Initial research categories

- character / anime / children's telephone services
- fortune-telling / consultation
- music / entertainment
- games / novelty
- adult / sexual content
- two-shot / encounter services
- Internet connection / dialer incidents
- disaster donation and public-interest uses
- family conflict / high bills
- public telephone use
- magazines and advertisements as discovery channels
- memories of the sound, waiting, dialing, and telephone itself

## Research question

> Before smartphones and the Web became everyday personal media, how did people experience paid, telephone-based information as a private or semi-private medium inside a shared household telephone culture?

## Important distinction

Do not collapse these statements:

1. "This happened historically." — requires evidence.
2. "People remember this happening." — the recollection itself is evidence.
3. "This is a plausible interpretation." — research hypothesis.

The RAG corpus should preserve all three layers.

## Dial P2 hypothesis

The project name **Dial P2** is a contemporary reinterpretation, not a historical name. A working concept is `P2 = Person ↔ Person`: collect memories of an old telephone medium and use them to study how people connected to information and to other people.
